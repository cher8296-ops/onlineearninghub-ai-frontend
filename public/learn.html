Free practical learning

Build skills. Work wisely. Use AI responsibly.

A growing set of original, action-oriented lessons for people starting with digital skills, online work and AI-assisted productivity. No income promises or shortcuts.

1. Start with a skill—not a promise

Pick one service you can learn and demonstrate: writing/editing, translation, simple design, spreadsheet support, research assistance or basic web tasks. A clear sample of your work is more persuasive than claims of guaranteed earnings.

Try this today

  1. Choose one skill and one audience.
  2. Create a small practice sample.
  3. Ask a trusted person for feedback and revise it.

2. A realistic online-work workflow

  1. Define a narrow service and deliverable.
  2. Build two or three portfolio samples without inventing client experience.
  3. Research opportunities on reputable platforms and verify the company and terms.
  4. Apply with a specific message tailored to the role.
  5. Track applications, responses, learning and follow-up dates.

3. Use AI as an assistant, not as proof

AI can help brainstorm, outline, explain, draft and review. It may fabricate facts, references, job postings or credentials. Check important claims against primary sources and rewrite output in your own voice.

Prompt pattern

“Teach me [skill] at beginner level. Give one short explanation, a worked example, a practice task, a checklist for checking my answer, and common mistakes. Avoid invented facts.”

4. Protect yourself from scams

  • Be wary of upfront fees for a job, “unlocking” earnings or mandatory deposits.
  • Do not share passwords, verification codes, identity documents or card details with unknown contacts.
  • Verify recruiters using the official company website and email domain.
  • Search for independent reports; do not trust screenshots of payment alone.
  • Pause if pressured to act urgently or move money/crypto for someone else.

5. Make a portfolio that earns trust

Show the problem, your process, the finished sample and what you learned. Label practice projects honestly. Use only assets you have permission to publish and remove personal details from examples.

6. Weekly progress review

  • What did I practice and finish?
  • What evidence of quality did I create?
  • What applications or outreach did I send?
  • What response or lesson did I get?
  • What is the one measurable next action?

07

Long-form videos & Shorts

Long-form can support mid-roll ad breaks on monetized videos that are at least 8 minutes long, but ad slots are not guaranteed to serve. Shorts use a separate Shorts Feed revenue-sharing model. Use Shorts to reach new viewers and long-form for deeper teaching when it suits the audience.

Know the metrics / Safartuu sirriitti hubadhu: CPM is what advertisers pay per 1,000 ad impressions; RPM is the creator's revenue per 1,000 views after YouTube's revenue share and across eligible revenue sources. They are not interchangeable. CPM fi RPM wal fakkaatan miti; RPM galii qabiyyee uumaa irraa argamu agarsiisa, CPM immoo baasii beeksisaa agarsiisa.

Build a sustainable content system / Sirna qabiyyee itti fufiinsa qabu ijaari

  1. Choose one niche at the intersection of your knowledge, audience need and responsible monetization.
  2. Write 3–5 useful subtopics and verify claims with primary sources.
  3. Publish original, accessible videos with accurate titles, captions and clear audio.
  4. Review audience retention, returning viewers, geography, CPM and RPM in YouTube Analytics; improve based on evidence.
  5. Disclose sponsorships and affiliate links, respect copyright, and follow platform monetization policies.

Beekumsa kee, fedhii daawwataa fi hojii itti gaafatamummaa qabu walitti fidii mata-duree filadhu. Qabiyyee mataa keetii uumi; madda mirkaneessi; bu'aa YouTube Analytics irratti hundaa'uun fooyyessi; beeksisa kaffalame ibsi; mirga abbummaa fi seera platformii kabaji.

Official YouTube references / Maddoota YouTube: Understand CPM and RPM · Mid-roll ads for videos 8+ minutes · Shorts monetization policies.
