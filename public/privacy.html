Privacy Notice

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This notice explains how OnlineEarningHub AI handles information when account, AI history, feedback, analytics and payment features are enabled. Some features remain unavailable until their required database, email and payment-provider configuration is completed.

Information we collect

  • Account details: email address, password hash (never the plain-text password), verification status and subscription tier.
  • AI activity: prompts, generated responses, tool names, model labels and timestamps when you use the AI tools while signed in.
  • Feedback: the rating and optional message you submit.
  • Analytics: allow-listed event names and page paths. We do not intentionally store IP addresses or prompt text in analytics events.
  • Billing references: payment-provider customer/subscription identifiers and subscription status. Card details are handled by the payment provider, not stored by this website.

How information is used

We use this information to authenticate accounts, verify email addresses, provide account history and usage limits, respond to feedback, operate subscriptions, secure the service and understand aggregate usage.

AI processing

Prompts are sent to configured AI providers to generate responses. Depending on provider availability and configuration, these may include Google Gemini and OpenAI. Provider processing and retention are governed by their own policies. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers or confidential information in prompts.

Email and payments

If email delivery is configured, we send account verification and essential service messages. If a payment provider is configured, checkout and subscription events are processed by that provider. Premium access is enabled only after a verified payment-provider event. Payment provider policies also apply.

Storage and retention

Account and AI history data are stored in the service database when that feature is provisioned. Browser theme and up to 12 recent guest activities may also be stored locally on your device. Data is retained while needed to operate the service, meet security and accounting obligations, or resolve disputes. A self-service account deletion and data export feature is not yet available; do not use the service for information requiring guaranteed deletion.

Security

Passwords are stored as salted, computationally expensive hashes. Session tokens are signed and expire. We apply access controls to account and administrator routes, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Sharing

We share necessary data with hosting, database, email and AI providers, and payment providers when enabled. We do not sell personal data. Providers process data under their own terms and privacy notices.

Your choices and contact

You can avoid submitting optional feedback and can stop using the service. Browser-local history can be cleared in the website or by clearing browser storage. For account-data requests, use the verified support contact published by the service operator; a dedicated support contact must be published before broad commercial launch.

Changes

This notice may change as features and providers are enabled. The date above indicates the latest revision.

This is a general product privacy notice, not jurisdiction-specific legal advice. Obtain a local legal review before commercial launch.
