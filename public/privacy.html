Privacy Notice

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This notice describes the current guest-access version of OnlineEarningHub AI. It does not describe account, subscription or payment features; those are not currently enabled.

Information you enter

When you submit a prompt, the text is sent to our backend service so it can request an AI response. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, government identifiers, confidential business information or other sensitive personal data.

AI service providers

To generate responses, the backend may send your prompt to configured AI providers, including Google Gemini and, when configured as a fallback, OpenAI. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. A fallback provider may be used when the primary provider is unavailable.

Browser storage

The current website stores your theme preference and up to 12 recent prompt/response entries in your browser's local storage. This is local to the browser and device profile you use; it is not an account-based cloud history. You can clear recent activity in the website or clear this site's browser storage. Clearing browser storage may remove this information.

Accounts, payments and tracking

The current release does not provide user registration, login, payment processing or subscription management. We do not claim that analytics or advertising trackers are active. Hosting providers may process technical request information as part of operating and securing the service.

Retention and security

Browser history remains until you clear it or browser data is removed. Prompt processing and server/AI-provider retention are subject to the applicable hosting and provider policies. We do not promise deletion from third-party provider systems through this website.

Children and changes

This general-purpose service is not designed to collect information from children. This notice may be updated as features change; check this page for the current version.

This is a plain-language product notice, not legal advice. A verified support contact and jurisdiction-specific review should be added before commercial launch.
